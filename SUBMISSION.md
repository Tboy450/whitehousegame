# White House Arcade submission inquiry

Research checked September 8, 2026. This document is a draft; no message has been sent.

The [official Arcade page](https://www.whitehouse.gov/arcade/) lists its games and a coming-soon slot. It does not display an open submission program or a game-specific submission form. The available general route is the [White House contact form](https://www.whitehouse.gov/contact/). Asking that office for the appropriate review channel does not establish that unsolicited games are accepted.

## Documented subject overlap

The [August 20, 2026 National Space Transportation Policy](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/08/national-security-presidential-memorandum-nspm-17/) directs NASA to facilitate commercial lunar transportation and explore commercial robotic and human transportation to Mars. [SpaceX's Starship overview](https://new.spacex.com/vehicles/starship) describes a reusable transportation system for Earth orbit, the Moon, and Mars, including booster return and orbital refilling.

Those sources establish an overlap in space transportation objectives. They do not establish a joint endorsement of this game or agreement on other policies. Booster recovery and orbital refilling are factual space-technology subjects that could inform a future educational mechanic. Such mechanics are not implemented in the current runner.
