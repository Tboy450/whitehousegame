File: `rocket-duo.png` (1536 × 1024, RGBA). Generated once using OpenAI's built-in image_gen tool, then copied into this project with its original alpha channel. No external runtime image dependency.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: single transparent PNG player sprite for a polished detailed Chrome dinosaur style side-scrolling pixel arcade game.

Primary request: One HORIZONTAL silver retro rocket pointing RIGHT, with Donald Trump and Elon Musk BOTH seated astride the TOP of the SAME rocket. Exactly two adult riders, both fully visible and immediately recognizable as friendly caricatures. Trump is seated at the front/right, wearing a navy suit, white shirt and bright red tie, with distinctive blond swoop hair. Elon is seated behind him/left, with short dark hair and a black T-shirt. Both riders hold rocket handles and have their legs straddling the rocket. Their large expressive heads must read clearly when the entire combined sprite is displayed at about 220 pixels wide.

Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent alpha background, no environment.

Style/medium: polished chunky 16-bit pixel art, deliberate crisp square pixel clusters, strong charcoal pixel outlines, controlled stepped shading, clean readable silhouette.

Composition/framing: landscape canvas; single isolated combined rocket-and-two-riders sprite centered with tight transparent margins of about 5%; strictly side profile, rightward motion, rocket perfectly level horizontally with no perspective tilt. Complete faces, heads, bodies and rocket within canvas. Trump in front/right and Elon behind/left.

Color palette/materials: warm off-white silver rocket body, vermilion red nose cone and fins, one teal window, small orange jet flame trailing to the LEFT; palette complements a pale ivory desert runner game.

Constraints: exactly one rocket and exactly two adults riding together on top of that rocket; distinct non-overlapping readable heads, small enough bodies and short seated legs for a compact unified sprite. True transparency must surround the entire sprite and fill empty spaces between shapes.

Avoid: background, ground, shadows on a ground plane, scenery, separate portraits, extra people, anyone standing alongside, vertical or tilted rocket, photorealism, smooth vector curves, gradients, blurry edges, text, letters, logos, watermarks, UI, border, sprite sheet, multiple poses or variants.
